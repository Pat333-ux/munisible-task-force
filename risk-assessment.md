## I. Purpose
This document defines the **official risk assessment framework** used by the Munisible Task Force (MTF) within Beast System 3.0.  
It establishes the criteria, indicators, thresholds, and escalation pathways for identifying, classifying, and documenting risks across municipal, operational, wellbeing, civil rights, and systemic domains.

See: **[Risk Register](ca://s?q=Describe_risk_register)**

---

## II. Risk Assessment Principles
All risk assessments must adhere to the following principles:

- **Neutrality** — Assessments must be factual, non‑interpretive, and bias‑free.  
- **Non‑Coercion** — Risk identification must never be used for punitive or enforcement actions.  
- **Wellbeing Priority** — Human safety and trauma‑prevention override all operational concerns.  
- **Civil Rights Protection** — Risks involving rights violations trigger automatic escalation.  
- **DAO Anchoring** — All assessments must be hash‑verified and anchored to the ledger.  
- **Traceability** — Every risk decision must be auditable and lineage‑linked.

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**

---

## III. Risk Domains
The MTF recognizes **six sovereign risk domains**:

1. **Operational Risk** — Failures in municipal or agent processes.  
2. **Compliance Risk** — Violations of governance, legal, or procedural standards.  
3. **Financial Risk** — Resource mismanagement or misuse of public funds.  
4. **Digital & Cybersecurity Risk** — System integrity, data breaches, or unauthorized access.  
5. **Reputational Risk** — Loss of public trust or ethical violations.  
6. **Ceremonial & Lineage Risk** — Breaks in authorship lineage, integrity chain, or DAO sovereignty.

See: **[Domain Model](ca://s?q=Describe_domain_model)**

## IV. Risk Indicators

### A. Operational Indicators
- Documentation inconsistencies  
- Failure to follow workflows  
- Missing timestamps or hashes  
- Municipal service failures  

### B. Compliance Indicators
- Policy violations  
- Unauthorized access  
- Missing DAO anchors  
- Non‑compliant municipal actions  

### C. Wellbeing Indicators
- Emotional distress  
- Physical harm risk  
- Unsafe conditions  
- Trauma‑triggering interactions  

See: **[Victim Support Protocols](ca://s?q=Write_victim_support_protocols)**

### D. Civil Rights Indicators
- Privacy violations  
- Discrimination  
- Coercion  
- Due process concerns  

### E. Telemetry Indicators
- Anomaly spikes  
- Pattern matches to known risks  
- Systemic irregularities  

See: **[Telemetry Config](ca://s?q=Explain_telemetry_config)**

### F. Integrity Indicators
- Hash mismatches  
- Lineage discontinuity  
- Ledger anchor gaps  

See: **[Blockchain Integrity Log](ca://s?q=Explain_blockchain_integrity_log)**

## V. Risk Levels

### **Low Risk**
- Minor documentation issues  
- No wellbeing or rights concerns  
- No systemic indicators  

### **Moderate Risk**
- Repeated operational issues  
- Early wellbeing indicators  
- Minor rights‑impact concerns  

### **Elevated Risk**
- Confirmed rights‑impact  
- Confirmed wellbeing risk  
- Telemetry anomalies  

### **High Risk**
- Misconduct indicators  
- Systemic pattern matches  
- Integrity chain irregularities  

### **Critical Risk**
- Severe rights violations  
- Severe wellbeing harm  
- Systemic municipal failure  
- Ledger integrity threat  

See: **[Incident Escalation](ca://s?q=Explain_incident_escalation)**
