## I. What This System Is
Beast System 3.0 is a **non‑punitive, rights‑protective, wellbeing‑centered governance system** designed to document municipal behavior, protect vulnerable populations, and ensure transparency across public‑facing institutions.  
It is **not** law enforcement, **not** surveillance, and **not** a disciplinary system.  
It is a **documentation, oversight, and wellbeing protection framework**.

See: **[System Architecture](ca://s?q=Describe_system_architecture)**

---

## II. Purpose of the Munisible Task Force
The Munisible Task Force (MTF) exists to:

- Document municipal interactions  
- Protect civil and constitutional rights  
- Identify wellbeing risks  
- Detect misconduct patterns  
- Provide non‑coercive oversight  
- Anchor evidence and governance artifacts to the DAO ledger  

The MTF does **not** punish, enforce, or coerce.  
Its role is **transparency, protection, and documentation**.

See: **[Legal Basis](ca://s?q=Describe_legal_basis)**

---

## III. What the System Actually Does
The system performs four core functions:

### 1. **Documentation**
Records municipal interactions, environmental conditions, and wellbeing indicators.

### 2. **Verification**
Ensures all records are accurate, timestamped, hash‑verified, and immutable.

### 3. **Analysis**
Identifies patterns of harm, instability, discrimination, or systemic failure.

### 4. **Oversight**
Routes concerning events to independent oversight and, when required, to Executive Authority.

See: **[Workflow Diagram](ca://s?q=Describe_workflow_diagram)**

---

## IV. What the System Does *Not* Do
Beast System 3.0 is legally and structurally prohibited from:

- Arrests  
- Detentions  
- Searches  
- Seizures  
- Surveillance  
- Punitive actions  
- Coercive questioning  
- Discriminatory behavior  

These prohibitions cannot be overridden.

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**
