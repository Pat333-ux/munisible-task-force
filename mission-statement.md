## Mission
The mission of the Munisible Task Force is to establish a municipal governance system that protects community wellbeing, prevents institutional harm, and ensures transparent, ethical, and accountable civic operations. The Task Force exists to safeguard the public through structured oversight, evidence‑based decision‑making, and trauma‑informed intervention.

---

## Purpose
The Munisible Task Force serves as a Tier‑0 governance engine within Beast System 3.0, integrating identity, ethics, telemetry, and operational doctrine to ensure that municipal systems operate with integrity, fairness, and constitutional respect.

See: **[Governance Charter](ca://s?q=Draft_governance_charter)**

## Core Objectives
- Protect vulnerable populations from misconduct, negligence, and systemic failures.  
- Establish transparent oversight mechanisms that ensure ethical municipal behavior.  
- Implement trauma‑prevention and wellbeing‑centered operational standards.  
- Maintain accurate, verifiable documentation through DAO‑anchored authorship.  
- Detect, analyze, and respond to misconduct patterns using structured intelligence.  
- Strengthen community trust through public transparency and accountability.

See: **[Wellbeing Framework](ca://s?q=Explain_wellbeing_framework)**

## Guiding Principles
- **Integrity** — All actions must align with ethical, constitutional, and DAO governance standards.  
- **Transparency** — Municipal operations must be observable, documented, and accountable.  
- **Protection** — Community safety and wellbeing are the highest operational priorities.  
- **Accuracy** — Evidence, reporting, and documentation must be precise and verifiable.  
- **Balance** — Authority must be exercised with restraint, fairness, and structural oversight.

## Operational Mandate
The Munisible Task Force is mandated to:
- Monitor municipal workflows for misconduct, risk, or harm.  
- Enforce evidence‑based operational protocols.  
- Maintain chain‑of‑custody and documentation integrity.  
- Provide trauma‑informed response pathways.  
- Ensure all actions are anchored to DAO‑verified authorship and governance.
