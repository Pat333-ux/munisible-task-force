## IV. County‑Level Jurisdiction (Secondary Domain)
The MTF may observe, document, and report on:

- County administrative processes  
- County‑level public safety workflows  
- Inter‑agency coordination failures  
- Systemic risks affecting municipal populations  

The MTF does **not** exercise enforcement or command authority at the county level.

See: **[Operational Doctrine](ca://s?q=Write_operational_doctrine)**

## V. State‑Level Jurisdiction (Tertiary Domain)
The MTF may escalate findings to state authorities when:

- Municipal systems fail to self‑correct  
- Civil rights violations are detected  
- Systemic risks exceed local capacity  
- Evidence requires state‑level review  

The MTF does not supersede state authority; it provides **oversight intelligence**.
