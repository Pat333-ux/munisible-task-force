## IV. Workflow Integrity Rules

- No step may be skipped.  
- All transitions must be logged.  
- All evidence must be hashed and anchored.  
- All rights-impact indicators trigger escalation.  
- All wellbeing indicators override operational convenience.  
- No workflow may generate punitive or coercive actions.
