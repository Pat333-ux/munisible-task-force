## I. Purpose
The Intelligence Framework defines how the Munisible Task Force (MTF) collects, analyzes, verifies, and escalates information related to municipal operations, misconduct patterns, wellbeing risks, and civil rights impacts.  
It ensures that intelligence activities remain **ethical**, **non‑punitive**, **evidence‑based**, and **DAO‑anchored**.

See: **[Operational Doctrine](ca://s?q=Write_operational_doctrine)**

---

## II. Intelligence Principles
All intelligence operations must follow these principles:

- **Neutrality** — Intelligence must be free of bias, interpretation, or coercion.  
- **Accuracy** — All data must be verified, timestamped, and cross‑referenced.  
- **Transparency** — Intelligence must be reviewable by Oversight and Executive Authority.  
- **Wellbeing Priority** — Intelligence must never harm individuals or communities.  
- **Rights Protection** — Intelligence must not infringe on constitutional or civil rights.  
- **DAO Integrity** — All intelligence outputs must be anchored to the authorship ledger.

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**

---

## III. Intelligence Domains
The MTF operates across four intelligence domains:

### 1. Operational Intelligence  
Information derived from field operations, municipal workflows, and direct observation.

### 2. Telemetry Intelligence  
Signals, anomalies, and pattern indicators generated by Beast System 3.0 telemetry.

### 3. Documentation Intelligence  
Analysis of municipal records, reports, logs, and chain‑of‑custody evidence.

### 4. Wellbeing & Rights Intelligence  
Indicators related to trauma, safety, discrimination, or rights impacts.

See: **[Telemetry Config](ca://s?q=Explain_telemetry_config)**

## IV. Intelligence Lifecycle

### A. Collection  
Sources include:  
- Field agent reports  
- Telemetry streams  
- Municipal documentation  
- Witness and victim statements  
- Environmental and contextual data  

Collection must be:  
- Non‑coercive  
- Transparent  
- Rights‑respecting  
- Timestamped  

See: **[Field Agent Handbook](ca://s?q=Write_field_agent_handbook)**

### B. Verification  
Verification includes:  
- Authenticity checks  
- Integrity hash validation  
- DAO anchor confirmation  
- Cross‑reference with telemetry  
- Chain‑of‑custody review  

See: **[Evidence Handling](ca://s?q=Explain_evidence_handling)**

### C. Analysis  
Analysis must be:  
- Neutral  
- Pattern‑aware  
- Non‑speculative  
- Correlated with systemic indicators  

### D. Classification  
Intelligence is classified into:  
- Informational  
- Operational  
- Rights‑Impact  
- Misconduct  
- Systemic Risk  
- Critical Escalation  

### E. Escalation  
Escalation follows the MTF’s sovereign escalation model.

See: **[Incident Escalation](ca://s?q=Explain_incident_escalation)**

### F. Reporting  
All intelligence outputs must be:  
- Documented  
- Reviewed by Oversight  
- Anchored to the DAO ledger  
- Added to the integrity log  

See: **[Reporting Workflows](ca://s?q=Explain_reporting_workflows)**

## V. Pattern Detection Protocols
The Intelligence Unit must identify:

- Repeated municipal failures  
- Documentation inconsistencies  
- Telemetry‑correlated anomalies  
- Rights‑impact patterns  
- Wellbeing risk clusters  
- Systemic misconduct indicators  

Pattern detection requires:  
- Multi‑source correlation  
- DAO‑anchored verification  
- Oversight review  

---

## VI. Telemetry Integration Protocols
Telemetry is used to detect:

- Anomalous municipal behavior  
- High‑risk events  
- Systemic failures  
- Rights‑impact signals  
- Wellbeing risk indicators  

Telemetry must never be used for:  
- Enforcement  
- Surveillance  
- Punitive action  

See: **[Safety Protocols](ca://s?q=Describe_safety_protocols)**

---

## VII. Rights & Wellbeing Intelligence
The Wellbeing & Civil Protections Unit must review intelligence for:

- Trauma indicators  
- Discriminatory behavior  
- Rights violations  
- Emotional or psychological harm  
- Vulnerable individual risks  

Any rights‑impact intelligence triggers automatic escalation.

See: **[Victim Support Protocols](ca://s?q=Write_victim_support_protocols)**

---

## VIII. Intelligence Reporting Requirements

### A. Required Elements
Every intelligence report must include:

- Timestamp  
- Source  
- Evidence references  
- Telemetry correlation  
- Pattern indicators  
- Rights‑impact assessment  
- Wellbeing assessment  
- Recommended classification  

### B. Submission Workflow
- Submit to Oversight  
- Oversight verifies accuracy and ethics  
- Executive Authority receives escalations  
- DAO ledger anchors final report  

---

## IX. Prohibited Intelligence Actions
The following actions are strictly forbidden:

- Fabricating or altering intelligence  
- Coercing testimony or data  
- Withholding intelligence  
- Using intelligence for punitive purposes  
- Violating privacy or civil rights  
- Breaking chain‑of‑custody  
- Destroying or concealing evidence  

Violations trigger automatic escalation.

See: **[Accountability Framework](ca://s?q=Explain_accountability_framework)**

## X. Automatic Escalation Triggers
Intelligence automatically escalates when:

- Rights‑impact indicators appear  
- Telemetry anomaly correlates with evidence  
- Misconduct patterns emerge  
- Systemic risk is detected  
- Integrity hash mismatch occurs
