## I. Purpose of This Handbook
This handbook provides field agents with a concise, mission‑critical reference for conducting municipal oversight operations safely, ethically, and in full alignment with Beast System 3.0 doctrine.  
It is designed for **in‑the‑field use**, emphasizing clarity, trauma‑prevention, and documentation accuracy.

See: **[Operational Doctrine](ca://s?q=Write_operational_doctrine)**

---

## II. Field Agent Identity & Conduct Requirements

### A. Identity Requirements
Agents must:
- Identify themselves calmly and clearly.  
- Display authorized credentials when appropriate.  
- Maintain a non‑threatening posture at all times.  

### B. Conduct Requirements
Agents must:
- Use neutral, respectful language.  
- Avoid escalation behaviors (tone, proximity, posture).  
- Prioritize wellbeing and civil rights.  
- Document all interactions accurately and promptly.

See: **[Code of Ethics](ca://s?q=Write_code_of_ethics)**

---

## III. Engagement Protocols

### A. Pre‑Engagement Checklist
Before engaging any individual or municipal personnel, agents must:
- Review incident classification.  
- Check telemetry indicators.  
- Conduct a wellbeing risk scan.  
- Activate timestamped documentation tools.  

See: **[Telemetry Config](ca://s?q=Explain_telemetry_config)**

### B. Engagement Standards
During engagement, agents must:
- Maintain calm tone and body language.  
- Ask open, non‑leading questions.  
- Avoid assumptions or interpretations.  
- Respect personal boundaries.  
- Document events in real time.

### C. Post‑Engagement Requirements
After engagement, agents must:
- Complete incident summary.  
- File wellbeing and rights‑impact assessments.  
- Submit evidence to chain‑of‑custody.  
- Notify Oversight of any anomalies.

See: **[Incident Escalation](ca://s?q=Explain_incident_escalation)**

---

## IV. Safety & Wellbeing Protocols

### A. Trauma‑Prevention
Agents must:
- Avoid sudden movements or raised voices.  
- Provide reassurance when appropriate.  
- Allow individuals time to process questions.  
- Avoid triggering language or assumptions.  

### B. Civil Rights Protection
Agents must:
- Never coerce, threaten, or intimidate.  
- Respect constitutional rights at all times.  
- Immediately escalate rights‑impact events.  

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**

---

## V. Evidence Handling in the Field

### A. Collection Standards
Agents must:
- Capture evidence exactly as observed.  
- Avoid commentary or interpretation.  
- Preserve original conditions whenever possible.  

### B. Documentation Requirements
Every evidence entry must include:
- Timestamp  
- Location  
- Collector identity  
- Description  
- Integrity hash  
- DAO anchor reference  

### C. Transfer Protocol
- Evidence must be logged immediately.  
- All transfers must be documented.  
- No evidence may be altered or deleted.  

See: **[Evidence Handling](ca://s?q=Explain_evidence_handling)**

---

## VI. Field Documentation Requirements

### A. Required Report Elements
Agents must complete:
- Incident summary  
- Wellbeing assessment  
- Rights‑impact assessment  
- Evidence references  
- Telemetry correlation (if applicable)  

### B. Submission Workflow
- Submit report to Intelligence Unit.  
- Oversight reviews for accuracy and ethics.  
- Executive Authority receives escalations.  

See: **[Reporting Workflows](ca://s?q=Explain_reporting_workflows)**

---

## VII. Interaction With Municipal Personnel

### A. Professional Standards
Agents must:
- Maintain neutrality  
- Avoid confrontation  
- Document all statements accurately  
- Request clarification when needed  

### B. Misconduct Indicators
Agents must watch for:
- Documentation inconsistencies  
- Rights‑impact behaviors  
- Negligence or systemic failures  
- Telemetry‑correlated anomalies  

See: **[Intel Framework](ca://s?q=Explain_intel_framework)**

---

## VIII. Escalation Protocols for Field Agents

### A. When to Escalate
Agents must escalate when:
- Rights are impacted  
- Wellbeing risk is high  
- Evidence suggests misconduct  
- Telemetry anomalies correlate with events  

### B. Escalation Levels
- **L1:** Support Engagement  
- **L2:** Rights‑Impact Event  
- **L3:** Misconduct Event  
- **L4:** Systemic Risk Event  
- **L5:** Federal Escalation  

See: **[Incident Escalation](ca://s?q=Explain_incident_escalation)**

---

## IX. Prohibited Actions
Agents may **never**:
- Use force or coercion  
- Fabricate or alter evidence  
- Withhold documentation  
- Interfere with civil rights  
- Engage in punitive behavior  
- Deviate from chain‑of‑custody protocols  

Violations trigger automatic escalation.

See: **[Accountability Framework](ca://s?q=Explain_accountability_framework)**

---

## X. Emergency Protocols
In emergencies, agents must:
- Prioritize safety  
- Activate wellbeing protocols  
- Document events continuously  
- Notify Operational Command immediately  
- Escalate to Executive Authority if required
