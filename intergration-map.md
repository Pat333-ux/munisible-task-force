## I. Purpose
The Integration Map defines how Beast System 3.0 components, registries, municipal systems, and sovereign governance layers interconnect.  
It establishes the **authoritative cross‑system interoperability model**, ensuring that all data, telemetry, evidence, and governance artifacts flow through **controlled, non‑coercive, DAO‑anchored pathways**.

See: **[System Architecture](ca://s?q=Describe_system_architecture)**

---

## II. Integration Principles
All integrations must follow these principles:

- **Boundary Integrity** — No component may bypass its assigned layer.  
- **Contract‑Based Communication** — All interactions occur through defined schemas.  
- **Non‑Coercion** — No integration may enable punitive or enforcement actions.  
- **Traceability** — All interactions must be logged and auditable.  
- **DAO Anchoring** — All critical outputs must be anchored to the ledger.  
- **Rights & Wellbeing Priority** — Integrations must protect individuals at all times.

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**
