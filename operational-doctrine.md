## I. Purpose of the Doctrine
The Operational Doctrine defines the governing logic, behavioral standards, and procedural expectations for all Munisible Task Force (MTF) personnel and automated systems.  
It ensures that every action taken aligns with municipal safety, ethical governance, and DAO‑anchored integrity.

See: **[Governance Charter](ca://s?q=Draft_governance_charter)**

---

## II. Operational Philosophy
The MTF operates under a **non‑punitive, non‑enforcement** model.  
Its purpose is oversight, protection, documentation, and wellbeing—not force, coercion, or control.

Core principles:
- De‑escalation  
- Trauma‑prevention  
- Accuracy and transparency  
- Constitutional respect  
- Community protection  

See: **[Code of Ethics](ca://s?q=Write_code_of_ethics)**

## III. Operational Scope
The MTF is responsible for:
- Monitoring municipal workflows

## V. Field Operations Doctrine
All field operations must follow these principles:

### A. Non‑Coercive Engagement  
Personnel must use calm, respectful, and non‑threatening communication.

### B. Safety‑First Protocol  
Safety of the public, personnel, and vulnerable individuals is the highest priority.

### C. Documentation Priority  
Every interaction must be documented accurately, neutrally, and promptly.

### D. Trauma‑Preventive Conduct  
Avoid triggering language, aggressive posture, or escalation behaviors.

See: **[Field Agent Handbook](ca://s?q=Write_field_agent_handbook)**

---

## VI. Incident Response Doctrine
Incidents must be handled using a structured, tiered response model:

### Tier 1 — Observation  
Monitor, document, and assess without intervention.

### Tier 2 — Engagement  
Provide support, information, or redirection when safe and appropriate.

### Tier 3 — Escalation  
Activate municipal partners only when required for safety or legal compliance.

### Tier 4 — Critical Response  
Trigger emergency protocols when imminent harm is present.

See: **[Incident Escalation](ca://s?q=Explain_incident_escalation)**
