## I. Purpose
This document establishes the mandatory standards for collecting, securing, documenting, transferring, analyzing, and anchoring evidence within the Munisible Task Force (MTF).  
It ensures that all evidence remains **neutral**, **accurate**, **tamper‑proof**, and **DAO‑anchored** throughout its lifecycle.

See: **[Operational Doctrine](ca://s?q=Write_operational_doctrine)**

---

## II. Evidence Integrity Principles
All evidence must adhere to the following principles:

- **Neutrality** — Evidence must be recorded exactly as observed.  
- **Accuracy** — No interpretation, alteration, or omission is permitted.  
- **Immutability** — All evidence must be anchored to the DAO ledger.  
- **Chain‑of‑Custody Protection** — Every transfer must be logged.  
- **Wellbeing & Rights Protection** — Evidence collection must not harm or violate rights.

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**

1. **Digital Evidence**

## IV. Evidence Collection Protocol

### A. Pre‑Collection Requirements
- Confirm safety and wellbeing of all individuals.  
- Activate timestamped documentation tools.  
- Ensure non‑coercive, trauma‑preventive conduct.  
- Verify that collection is lawful and rights‑respecting.

### B. Collection Standards
- Capture evidence in its original state.  
- Avoid commentary or interpretation.  
- Do not disturb physical evidence unless necessary for safety.  
- Document environmental context (time, location, conditions).

### C. Immediate Post‑Collection Actions
- Assign a unique evidence ID.  
- Log the collector’s identity and timestamp.  
- Secure the evidence in the chain‑of‑custody system.  
- Notify the Intelligence & Documentation Unit.

See: **[Field Agent Handbook](ca://s?q=Write_field_agent_handbook)**

## V. Chain‑of‑Custody Protocol

### A. Required Chain‑of‑Custody Elements
Each evidence entry must include:

- Evidence ID  
- Collector identity  
- Timestamp  
- Location  
- Description  
- Evidence type  
- Integrity hash  
- DAO anchor reference  
- Transfer history  

### B. Transfer Rules
- Every transfer must be logged.  
- Evidence may only be handled by authorized personnel.  
- No evidence may be removed, altered, or destroyed.  
- All transfers must include a new integrity hash.

### C. Storage Requirements
- Digital evidence must be stored in encrypted, access‑controlled systems.  
- Physical evidence must be stored in sealed, monitored containers.  
- All evidence must be redundantly backed up and DAO‑anchored.

See: **[Blockchain Integrity Log](ca://s?q=Explain_blockchain_integrity_log)**

## VI. Evidence Review Protocol

### A. Verification
The Intelligence Unit must verify:

- Authenticity  
- Completeness  
- Integrity hash match  
- DAO anchor match  
- Chain‑of‑custody continuity  

### B. Analysis
Analysis must be:

- Neutral  
- Non‑speculative  
- Pattern‑aware  
- Telemetry‑correlated  

### C. Rights & Wellbeing Review
The Wellbeing Unit must assess:

- Whether evidence indicates trauma or harm  
- Whether rights were impacted  
- Whether additional protections are required  

See: **[Victim Support Protocols](ca://s?q=Write_victim_support_protocols)**

## VII. Evidence Reporting Protocol

### A. Required Report Elements
Every evidence report must include:

- Summary of evidence  
- Integrity verification  
- Chain‑of‑custody log  
- Rights‑impact assessment  
- Wellbeing assessment  
- Telemetry correlation  
- Recommended classification  

### B. Submission Workflow
- Submit to Intelligence Unit  
- Oversight reviews for accuracy and ethics  
- Executive Authority receives escalations  
- DAO ledger anchors final report

See: **[Reporting Workflows](ca://s?q=Explain_reporting_workflows)**

## VIII. Prohibited Actions
The following actions are strictly forbidden:

- Altering evidence  
- Deleting evidence  
- Fabricating evidence  
- Coercing testimony  
- Withholding evidence  
- Breaking chain‑of‑custody  
- Failing to document transfers  
- Using evidence for punitive or coercive purposes  

Violations trigger automatic escalation.

See: **[Incident Escalation](ca://s?q=Explain_incident_escalation)**

## IX. Automatic Escalation Triggers
Evidence handling automatically escalates when:

- Integrity hash mismatch occurs  
- Chain‑of‑custody gap is detected  
- Rights‑impact indicators appear  
- Telemetry anomaly correlates with evidence  
- Evidence suggests misconduct or systemic failure
