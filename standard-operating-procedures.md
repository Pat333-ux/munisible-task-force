## I. Purpose of SOPs
These Standard Operating Procedures establish the required conduct, workflows, documentation rules, and safety protocols for all Munisible Task Force (MTF) personnel and automated systems.  
They ensure consistent, ethical, trauma‑preventive, and DAO‑anchored municipal oversight.

See: **[Operational Doctrine](ca://s?q=Write_operational_doctrine)**

---

## II. Scope
These SOPs apply to:  
- All MTF divisions and units  
- All municipal oversight activities  
- All documentation and evidence workflows  
- All interactions with the public, municipal staff, and vulnerable individuals  
- All telemetry, intelligence, and reporting systems  

See: **[Governance Structure](ca://s?q=Describe_governance_structure)**

---

## III. Core Operating Principles
All operations must follow these principles:

1. **Non‑Coercion** — No force, threats, intimidation, or punitive action.  
2. **Transparency** — All actions must be documented and reviewable.  
3. **Wellbeing Priority** — Trauma‑prevention and civil protections come first.  
4. **Accuracy** — Evidence and documentation must be precise and unaltered.  
5. **DAO Integrity** — All records must be anchored to the authorship registry.

See: **[Code of Ethics](ca://s?q=Write_code_of_ethics)**

## IV. Field Operations SOPs

### A. Pre‑Engagement Protocol
- Review incident context and telemetry indicators.  
- Conduct a wellbeing risk assessment.  
- Confirm non‑coercive engagement posture.  
- Ensure documentation tools are active and timestamped.

See: **[Telemetry Config](ca://s?q=Explain_telemetry_config)**

### B. Engagement Protocol
- Approach calmly and identify yourself.  
- Use non‑threatening, neutral language.  
- Maintain physical and emotional safety.  
- Avoid escalation behaviors (tone, posture, proximity).  
- Document all interactions in real time.

See: **[Field Agent Handbook](ca://s?q=Write_field_agent_handbook)**

### C. Post‑Engagement Protocol
- Complete incident documentation.  
- File wellbeing notes and rights‑impact assessments.  
- Submit evidence to the chain‑of‑custody system.  
- Notify Oversight of any anomalies or concerns.

---

## V. Incident Classification SOPs
Incidents must be classified into one of the following categories:

1. **Observation Event** — No direct engagement required.  
2. **Support Event** — Engagement required for wellbeing or clarity.  
3. **Rights‑Impact Event** — Civil protections triggered.  
4. **Misconduct Event** — Municipal or systemic failure detected.  
5. **Critical Event** — Imminent harm or high‑risk anomaly.

See: **[Incident Escalation](ca://s?q=Explain_incident_escalation)**

---

## VI. Documentation SOPs

### A. Required Documentation Elements
Every report must include:  
- Timestamp  
- Location  
- Personnel involved  
- Summary of events  
- Evidence references  
- Wellbeing assessment  
- Rights‑impact assessment  
- Telemetry correlation (if applicable)

### B. Chain‑of‑Custody Protocol
- Evidence must be logged immediately.  
- No edits, deletions, or alterations permitted.  
- All files must be DAO‑anchored.  
- Integrity logs must be updated automatically.

See: **[Evidence Handling](ca://s?q=Explain_evidence_handling)**

---

## VII. Wellbeing & Civil Protections SOPs

### A. Trauma‑Prevention Requirements
- Maintain calm tone and posture.  
- Avoid triggering language.  
- Respect personal boundaries.  
- Prioritize emotional stabilization.

### B. Civil Rights Protection Requirements
- No interference with constitutional rights.  
- No coercion or forced compliance.  
- Immediate escalation for rights‑impact events.

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**

---

## VIII. Intelligence & Reporting SOPs

### A. Evidence Analysis
- Validate authenticity.  
- Cross‑reference telemetry.  
- Identify patterns or anomalies.  
- Flag systemic risks.

### B. Reporting Workflow
- Submit reports to Intelligence Unit.  
- Oversight reviews for accuracy and ethics.  
- Executive Authority receives escalations.  
- DAO ledger anchors final findings.

See: **[Reporting Workflows](ca://s?q=Explain_reporting_workflows)**
