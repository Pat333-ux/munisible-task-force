## I. Purpose
This checklist defines the **mandatory municipal compliance requirements** for all operations, documentation, and oversight activities conducted by the Munisible Task Force (MTF).

The checklist is organized into six compliance domains:

1. Governance & Authority

3. Civil Rights & Wellbeing

# IV. Documentation & Records Compliance

### A. Documentation Standards
