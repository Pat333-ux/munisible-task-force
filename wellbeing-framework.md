## I. Purpose
The Wellbeing Framework defines the **sovereign standard of wellbeing** required for all operations within Beast System 3.0.  
It establishes the **rights‑based, trauma‑informed, non‑coercive** foundation that governs every interaction, assessment, and decision made by the Munisible Task Force (MTF).

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**

---

## II. Foundational Principles
The Wellbeing Framework is built on seven immutable principles:

1. **Dignity** — Every person is treated with inherent worth.  
2. **Safety** — Emotional, physical, and psychological safety are mandatory.  
3. **Autonomy** — No coercion, pressure, or forced compliance.  
4. **Stability** — Predictable, non‑threatening interactions.  
5. **Support** — Access to non‑punitive assistance.  
6. **Transparency** — Individuals understand what is documented about them.  
7. **Non‑Discrimination** — No bias based on housing, income, identity, or circumstance.

See: **[Safety Protocols](ca://s?q=Describe_safety_protocols)**

Beast System 3.0 defines **twelve sovereign wellbeing domains**, each representing a measurable dimension of human stability:

1. **Identity Stability**  
2. **Emotional Regulation**

5. **Family & Social Stability**  
6. **Community Integration**  
7. **Digital Wellbeing**

10. **Spiritual/Psychological Grounding**

These domains align with the Beast System 3.0 wellbeing model.

## IV. Wellbeing Indicators

### A. Positive Indicators
- Calm emotional state  
- Predictable environment  
- Respectful interactions  
- Access to support  
- Stable relationships  
- Safe physical conditions  

### B. Distress Indicators
- Emotional overwhelm  
- Fear or intimidation  
- Unsafe environment  
- Confusion or disorientation  
- Social isolation  
- Rights‑impact concerns  

### C. Crisis Indicators
- Immediate physical danger  
- Severe emotional distress  
- Coercive municipal behavior  
- Discrimination or harassment  
- Trauma‑triggering conditions  

See: **[Victim Support Protocols](ca://s?q=Write_victim_support_protocols)**
