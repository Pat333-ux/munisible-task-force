## I. Purpose
These Safety Protocols establish the mandatory standards for protecting the wellbeing, rights, and physical security of all individuals during Munisible Task Force (MTF) operations.  
They ensure that all actions taken by personnel and automated systems are **non‑coercive**, **trauma‑preventive**, and **aligned with DAO‑anchored governance**.

See: **[Operational Doctrine](ca://s?q=Write_operational_doctrine)**

---

## II. Safety Principles
All safety actions must follow these principles:

- **Safety First** — Human wellbeing overrides operational convenience.  
- **Non‑Coercion** — No force, threats, intimidation, or punitive behavior.  
- **Transparency** — All safety actions must be documented.  
- **Rights Protection** — Constitutional and civil rights must be preserved.  
- **Trauma Prevention** — Minimize emotional, psychological, and physical harm.  
- **DAO Integrity** — All safety events must be anchored to the authorship ledger.

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**

## III. Pre‑Operation Safety Protocols

### A. Pre‑Deployment Checklist
Before any field activity, agents must:

- Conduct a wellbeing risk scan  
- Review telemetry indicators  
- Confirm non‑coercive engagement posture  
- Activate timestamped documentation tools  
- Ensure communication channels are operational  

### B. Environmental Safety Assessment
Agents must evaluate:

- Physical hazards  
- Emotional or psychological risk factors  
- Presence of vulnerable individuals  
- Municipal personnel behavior patterns  

See: **[Telemetry Config](ca://s?q=Explain_telemetry_config)**

---

## IV. Engagement Safety Protocols

### A. Approach Standards
Agents must:

- Approach calmly and slowly  
- Maintain non‑threatening posture  
- Use neutral, respectful language  
- Respect personal boundaries  

### B. Communication Standards
Agents must:

- Avoid raised voices  
- Avoid accusatory or leading questions  
- Allow individuals time to respond  
- Provide reassurance when appropriate  

### C. Safety During Interaction
Agents must:

- Monitor wellbeing indicators  
- Avoid escalation behaviors  
- Document all safety‑relevant observations  
- Immediately de‑escalate if distress increases
