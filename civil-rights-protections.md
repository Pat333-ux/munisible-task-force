## I. Purpose
This document establishes the **civil rights protections** that govern all operations of the Munisible Task Force (MTF) and Beast System 3.0.  
These protections are **non‑negotiable**, **constitutionally grounded**, and **DAO‑anchored**, forming the ethical and legal perimeter of the entire system.

See: **[Legal Basis](ca://s?q=Describe_legal_basis)**

---

## II. Foundational Civil Rights Principles
All MTF operations must uphold:

- **Dignity** — Every individual is treated as a full human being with inherent worth.  
- **Equality** — No discrimination based on status, wealth, housing, identity, or circumstance.  
- **Autonomy** — Individuals retain full agency; no coercion is permitted.  
- **Due Process** — All documentation and actions must respect constitutional protections.  
- **Transparency** — Individuals have the right to know what is documented about them.  
- **Safety** — Emotional, physical, and psychological wellbeing must be protected at all times.

See: **[Safety Protocols](ca://s?q=Describe_safety_protocols)**

## III. Constitutional Anchors

### A. First Amendment
Protects:
- Documentation  
- Expression  
- Petitioning for redress  
- Organizational governance  

MTF must not interfere with speech, expression, or peaceful activity.

### B. Fourth Amendment
Prohibits:
- Intrusive data collection  
- Surveillance  
- Searches or seizures  

MTF may only collect **non‑coercive, voluntary, and contextual** information.

### C. Fifth & Fourteenth Amendments
Guarantee:
- Due process  
- Equal protection  
- Non‑discrimination  

These protections apply **regardless of housing status, income, or identity**.

### D. Eighth Amendment
Prohibits:
- Cruel or degrading treatment  
- Punitive or coercive actions  

MTF is structurally incapable of punitive behavior.

See: **[Incident Escalation](ca://s?q=Explain_incident_escalation)**

## IV. Prohibited Actions
The MTF is legally and structurally prohibited from:

- Arrests  
- Detentions  
- Searches  
- Seizures  
- Surveillance  
- Coercive questioning  
- Punitive actions  
- Threats or intimidation  
- Discriminatory treatment  
- Retaliation of any kind  

These prohibitions cannot be overridden by any unit or authority.

## VI. Rights‑Protection During Engagement

### A. Engagement Standards
Agents must:
- Use respectful, neutral language  
- Maintain non‑threatening posture  
- Respect personal boundaries  
- Avoid leading or coercive questions  
- Allow individuals time to respond  

### B. Documentation Standards
Documentation must:
- Be factual and neutral  
- Avoid assumptions or interpretations  
- Include timestamps and context  
- Protect privacy and dignity
