## I. Purpose
The Community Feedback Loop establishes the **sovereign, structured, non‑punitive mechanism** through which community members may submit praise, concerns, suggestions, or systemic observations.  
It ensures that all voices—especially vulnerable or marginalized individuals—are heard, documented, protected, and acted upon within Beast System 3.0.

See: **[Public Summary](ca://s?q=Describe_public_summary)**

---

## II. Foundational Principles
The feedback loop is governed by seven immutable principles:

1. **Accessibility** — Anyone may submit feedback without barriers.  
2. **Neutrality** — All submissions are treated with equal dignity.  
3. **Safety** — No retaliation, coercion, or punitive consequence is permitted.  
4. **Confidentiality** — Identity is protected unless voluntarily disclosed.  
5. **Traceability** — All submissions are logged, hashed, and lineage‑linked.  
6. **Transparency** — Submitters may request status updates.  
7. **Actionability** — Every submission must reach a documented outcome.

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**

---

## III. Feedback Categories
The system recognizes **four sovereign feedback categories**:

### 1. Praise
Acknowledgment of positive municipal or community behavior.

### 2. Concern
Non‑urgent issues requiring review or improvement.

### 3. Suggestion
Constructive proposals for system, municipal, or community enhancement.

### 4. Systemic Observation
Patterns, repeated failures, or structural issues requiring oversight.

See: **[Risk Assessment](ca://s?q=Describe_risk_assessment)**

---

## IV. Submission Methods
Feedback may be submitted through:

- Direct conversation with an MTF agent  
- Community kiosks  
- Municipal partners  
- Anonymous channels  
- Digital submission portals  

All methods must be:

- Non‑coercive  
- Accessible  
- Confidential  
- Rights‑respecting
