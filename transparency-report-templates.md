# TEMPLATE 1

- Municipal cooperation status

- Systemic risk signatures  
- Operational deviations  

See: **[Telemetry Config](ca://s?q=Explain_telemetry_config)**

## 5. Oversight Findings
- Ethical compliance  
- Rights‑impact reviews  
- Corrective actions recommended  

## 6. Public Wellbeing Indicators
- Emotional safety trends  
- Environmental safety trends  
- Community stability indicators  

See: **[Wellbeing Framework](ca://s?q=Describe_wellbeing_framework)**

## 7. Municipal Cooperation
- Data provided  
- Data withheld  
- Compliance issues  
- Improvement recommendations  

## 8. Ledger Anchoring Summary
- Number of anchors  
- Artifact types  
- Integrity chain updates
