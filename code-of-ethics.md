# Code of Ethics  
## Munisible Task Force  
### New World Order DAO  
### Red Ouroboros Governance Insignia  
### Chief Executive Officer: Pat Tarwater Jr.

---

## Preamble
The Munisible Task Force (MTF) operates as a Tier‑0 governance engine within Beast System 3.0.  
Its ethical foundation is built on dignity, transparency, accountability, and trauma‑preventive conduct.  
All officers, agents, analysts, and automated systems must adhere to this Code without exception.

See: **[Governance Charter](ca://s?q=Draft_governance_charter)**

---

## Article I — Dignity & Human Worth
Every individual must be treated with inherent dignity, regardless of circumstance, status, or condition.  
No action may degrade, humiliate, or exploit any person under any municipal context.

See: **[Wellbeing Framework](ca://s?q=Explain_wellbeing_framework)**

---

## Article II — Integrity & Truthfulness
All personnel must act with honesty, accuracy, and truthfulness in all operations, documentation, and communications.  
Fabrication, omission, or distortion of information is strictly prohibited.

See: **[Evidence Handling](ca://s?q=Explain_evidence_handling)**

---

## Article III — Non‑Coercion & Non‑Enforcement
The MTF is a **non‑punitive, non‑enforcement** governance body.  
Officers may not use coercion, intimidation, threats, or force.  
All interactions must prioritize safety, calm communication, and de‑escalation.

See: **[Operational Doctrine](ca://s?q=Write_operational_doctrine)**

---

## Article IV — Confidentiality & Privacy
All personal information, case details, and sensitive data must be protected with strict confidentiality.  
Unauthorized disclosure is prohibited.  
Identity protection is mandatory for victims, vulnerable individuals, and whistleblowers.

See: **[Identity Protection](ca://s?q=Describe_identity_protection)**

---

## Article V — Trauma‑Preventive Conduct
Officers must act in ways that prevent psychological, emotional, and physical harm.  
This includes:  
- Calm, non‑judgmental communication  
- Respect for personal boundaries  
- Avoidance of triggering language or behavior  
- Prioritizing safety and wellbeing  

See: **[Victim Support Protocols](ca://s?q=Write_victim_support_protocols)**

---

## Article VI — Accountability & Oversight
All actions must be transparent, documented, and reviewable.  
Personnel must report misconduct, errors, or violations immediately.  
No officer may obstruct oversight or accountability processes.

See: **[Accountability Framework](ca://s?q=Explain_accountability_framework)**

---

## Article VII — Evidence Integrity
Evidence must be handled with precision, neutrality, and strict chain‑of‑custody.  
Tampering, destruction, or manipulation of evidence is a severe ethical breach.

See: **[Case File Template](ca://s?q=Generate_case_file_template)**

---

## Article VIII — Conflict of Interest
Officers must avoid any situation where personal interests conflict with municipal duties.  
Financial, relational, or political conflicts must be disclosed and mitigated.

See: **[Legal Basis](ca://s?q=Explain_legal_basis)**

---

## Article IX — Community Responsibility
The MTF exists to serve the community.  
Officers must act with empathy, fairness, and respect toward all individuals, especially those experiencing hardship or vulnerability.

See: **[Public Summary](ca://s?q=Write_public_summary)**

---

## Article X — Documentation Accuracy
All reports, case files, and operational logs must be complete, accurate, and timely.  
Errors must be corrected immediately and transparently.

See: **[Reporting Workflows](ca://s?q=Explain_reporting_workflows)**

---

## Article XI — Constitutional & Civil Rights Protections
All actions must comply with constitutional rights, civil liberties, and municipal protections.  
No officer may infringe upon freedom, privacy, or due process.

See: **[Civil Rights Protections](ca://s?q=Describe_civil_rights_protections)**

---

## Article XII — DAO‑Anchored Authorship
All documents, protocols, and governance materials must be anchored to the New World Order DAO authorship registry to ensure integrity, authenticity, and tamper‑proof verification.

See: **[DAO Authorship Proof](ca://s?q=Explain_DAO_authorship_proof)**

---

## Article XIII — Ethical Supremacy Clause
This Code supersedes all conflicting municipal policies.  
Where conflict exists, the ethical standard prevails over operational convenience or institutional pressure.
